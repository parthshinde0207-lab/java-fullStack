Raj Thackeray believes that the identity of Maharashtra lies in its language, culture, history, and the pride of its people. He emphasizes that Marathi should not merely be celebrated on special occasions but should be preserved, promoted, and passed on to future generations. He often draws inspiration from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and believes that Maharashtra should progress without losing its cultural roots. His vision also includes supporting local people, protecting their interests, and ensuring that development benefits the people who call Maharashtra their home. He has spoken about promoting Marathi literature, art, and talent beyond the boundaries of the state. To him, progress is not only about modern buildings, roads, and infrastructure, but also about preserving the language, heritage, and identity that make Maharashtra unique.
